## Priority corrections

1. **Remove the river grid.** Break warm reflections into irregular horizontal fragments restricted to a few vertical columns beneath actual far-bank lights; vary width, spacing, brightness and gaps. Retain dark blue quiet water with a small number of cool horizontal highlights. No continuous orange lines or repeated wave lattice.
2. **Break and soften the paving ribbons.** Reduce opaque orange coverage substantially. Keep several bright small reflection cores, surrounded by weaker warm sheen; interrupt them with stone joints and uneven surfaces. Preserve underlying stone texture. The left foreground reflection should read as a broken vertical light path, not parallel painted bands.
3. **Keep the timber noise reduction**, then add restrained board-to-board variation, subtle edge wear and smaller knots. Avoid restoring the previous high-frequency grain.
4. Add the large, readable target details after reflections are corrected: left-wall drainpipe and cables, cage lantern silhouettes, cabin chimney, crate metal corners, then a few distant gables/chimneys.

This round is not a passing visual match. Brighter reflections alone do not compensate for their regular, opaque shape; fixing their structure is the highest-value next change.
