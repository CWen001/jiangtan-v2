## Prioritized corrections

1. **River and far bank, middle/right background:** replace the uninterrupted blue-gray water band with low-amplitude horizontal ripples and broken amber reflection columns aligned below lit windows. Develop the block skyline into an irregular row of dark pitched roofs, chimneys and articulated facades, with many small warm windows. Keep the existing shoreline position and crane silhouette.
2. **Foreground paving, especially lower left and beside the boat:** introduce selective low-roughness wet areas that reflect warehouse lamps and cabin windows. Carry narrow amber streaks toward the camera, interrupted by joints and rough patches. Preserve quieter stone faces; adding noise would not fix this gap.
3. **Warehouse ground floor and stacked crates, left:** reduce the broad saturated wall hotspots and extend softer warm light onto the ground. Give the nearly black crate faces readable dark-brown boards, restrained edge highlights, framing and small metal fasteners. Retain deep contact shadows without losing the visible construction.
4. **Facade, canopy and cabin surfaces:** replace fine granular breakup with larger painted tonal variation, clearer timber edges/plank separation and sparse localized plaster damage. The target's exposed masonry appears in selected worn patches, not across every surface. Add coherent dark linework at structural joints rather than blanket texture contrast.
5. **Lanterns and facade finishing:** use compact cage-shaped lantern silhouettes and warmer inner emitters; add the visible drainpipes, slack wall cables and understated frame/door hardware. These should follow the existing building surfaces and leave the walking route clear.

The camera and major object placement should be retained. The highest-return changes are reflective water, a convincing lit far shore, and selective wet amber reflections on the quay.
